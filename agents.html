What it does

One workspace, all local

UltroBot is an AI workspace I built and host myself. Everything runs privately on the UltroLab Server, with no paid APIs. Sign-in is invite only.

Chat

Like ChatGPT or Claude, with saved conversations for each person.

Make files

Ask for an image, PDF, Word doc, PowerPoint, audio clip, or video and it shows up right in the chat.

Coding agents

Architect, Frontend, Backend, and QA agents that plan, build, test, and security-review code.

Read your docs

Upload PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, or text files and have them summarized, answered, or rewritten.

Guard rails

Secret scanning and prompt-injection checks, and every push to GitHub needs a human's approval.

Private by default

Invite-only accounts, passkeys, and each person's chats and files stay theirs.

Now building

🎬 UltroBot Studio

The newest part of UltroBot turns a topic into a finished draft of a ~10-minute video for my YouTube channel. I type what the video is about, and UltroBot plans it, writes it, voices it, and edits it. I review everything before it goes anywhere.
