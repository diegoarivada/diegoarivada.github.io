What it is

UltroBot is an AI workspace I built and host myself. Everything runs privately on the UltroLab Server, with no paid APIs and nothing sent off to a cloud AI.

  • 💬 Chat like ChatGPT or Claude, with saved conversations
  • 🎨 Ask for an image, PDF, Word doc, PowerPoint, audio clip, or video and it shows up right in the chat
  • 📎 Upload PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, or text files and have them summarized, answered, or rewritten
  • 🛠️ AI coding agents (Architect, Frontend, Backend, and QA) that plan, build, test, and security-review code
  • 🛡️ Secret scanning and prompt-injection checks, and every push to GitHub needs a human's approval
  • 🔐 Username and password accounts, invite-only sign-up, and each person's chats and files stay private

🎬 Now building: UltroBot Studio

The newest part of UltroBot turns a topic into a finished draft of a ~10-minute video for my YouTube channel. I type what the video is about, and UltroBot plans it, writes it, voices it, and edits it. I review everything before it goes anywhere.

  • 📝 Plans an outline I can edit, then writes the script in my style, one chapter at a time
  • 🔎 Builds a fact checklist of every claim worth double checking, because AI gets facts wrong
  • 🎙️ Narrates in my own cloned voice, made only from my own recordings
  • 🎞️ Adds stock footage, captions, chapters, music, and a thumbnail
  • 📦 Posts every finished file to my Discord, so I can check it from my phone
  • 🛡️ Nothing publishes without my approval, and videos with a cloned voice get YouTube's AI disclosure

Where it's at: the script writer and fact checklist work, and I'm still tuning the voice to actually sound like me. Video rendering is next.

Want to try it yourself? I made a free, open-source version that runs on your own PC.
