About Me

I'm Diego Rivada, a Product Security Engineer and Backup Team Lead at The Boeing Company in Seattle, with 3+ years securing commercial aircraft systems. I lead security assessments, vulnerability management, and airline customer support to meet FAA and EASA airworthiness and cybersecurity requirements.

I’m currently pursuing my Master’s in Systems Architecting and Engineering at USC, deepening my understanding of complex systems and exploring how AI can enhance efficiency across engineering disciplines. I’m especially interested in how we can leverage AI not just to streamline workflows, but also to develop next-generation cybersecurity tools—particularly in fighting malware in smarter, more adaptive ways.

Beyond the technical work, I serve as our Backup Team Lead: helping set priorities, stepping in when our lead is away, mentoring and onboarding new engineers, and bridging engineering, compliance, and customer stakeholders to build secure, resilient systems.
